# Driver Onboarding Documents Checklist

The driver qualification (DQ) file is what an auditor, an insurer, or a
plaintiff's attorney asks for first after a crash. Under 49 CFR 391.51
most of these are legally required for employed drivers; fleets apply
the same file discipline to leased owner-operators.

## Identity and license

- [ ] Copy of current CDL, front and back, class and endorsements
      verified against the equipment the driver will pull (tanker,
      hazmat, doubles)
- [ ] Medical Examiner's Certificate (med card) copy, verified in the
      National Registry, expiry date calendared for renewal alerts
- [ ] CDLIS and MVR pull for every state the driver held a license in
      during the last 3 years, reviewed and signed by management
- [ ] Proof of legal work status and a second photo ID on file

## Federal qualification file (49 CFR 391)

- [ ] Employment application per 391.21, complete with 3 years of
      residence history and 10 years of employment history for CMV work
- [ ] Previous employer safety performance history checks sent to all
      DOT-regulated employers from the last 3 years (391.23), responses
      filed or good-faith attempts documented
- [ ] Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse pre-employment **full query** run
      and result filed (a limited query is not enough for hiring)
- [ ] Written Clearinghouse consent from the driver for full queries
- [ ] Pre-employment drug screen completed with a negative result
      **before** the first safety-sensitive duty (49 CFR 382.301)
- [ ] Standing consent forms for future drug and alcohol testing signed
- [ ] Road test completed and certificate filed (391.31), or a valid
      CDL accepted in place of the road test where the rule allows
- [ ] Certificate of violations and annual review forms calendared;
      both are annual requirements, not one-time paperwork (391.25)

## Company and safety onboarding

- [ ] Signed safety policy and handbook acknowledgment
- [ ] Hours-of-service and ELD training completed and logged
- [ ] Accident reporting procedure reviewed with the driver, with the
      in-cab accident kit confirmed present
- [ ] Cargo securement and, for reefer, temperature management training
      logged where applicable
- [ ] Emergency contact information collected
- [ ] Fuel card, toll transponder, and equipment issued, numbers recorded

## Pay and tax setup

- [ ] W-4 and state withholding forms for company drivers, or W-9 for
      leased owner-operators and contractors
- [ ] Direct deposit authorization or settlement instructions signed
- [ ] Per-diem election explained and documented if offered
- [ ] For leased owner-operators: signed lease agreement meeting
      49 CFR 376.12 (compensation, chargebacks, insurance duties,
      and the driver's right to review documents all in writing)
- [ ] Owner-operator insurance certificates: bobtail or non-trucking
      liability, physical damage, occupational accident where required

## File discipline

- [ ] Every expiry (CDL, med card, annual review, Clearinghouse annual
      query) is in a tickler system with 60, 30, and 7 day alerts
- [ ] DQ file stored separately from general HR files, access limited
- [ ] Retention rule noted on the file: DQ records are kept for the
      duration of employment plus 3 years (391.51)

This checklist is an operations aid, not legal advice. Confirm current
requirements against the FMCSA regulations for your operation type.
